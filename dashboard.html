FastApply

Your job search, one application at a time.

Paused
You review and submit every application. This helper fills supported fields in ordinary Safari tabs. It never clicks final submission or accepts legal terms. Employer pages may autosave entered data.

1. Your profile & resume

Edit saved local profile

Use null for unknown values. Put the absolute path to your PDF/DOCX in resume_path. Text values must use double quotes: "first_name": "Alex". Education and employment contain objects; skills contain quoted strings. Saving writes to the local file below and persists between launches. No credentials belong here.

2. Paste your SWElist email

All parsed jobs are included. No filtering by fit or location.

3. Application workspace

Pause/cancel takes effect between operations. Forms remain open. Resume job after handling login, questions, or an intermediate step. Existing values are preserved.

Employer accounts

When an employer asks you to register, use Safari's suggested password and save it in Passwords. If Safari offers Fill from email, select it in the verification field.

Scan and learn answers

Choose which answers to reuse. Employer answers stay scoped to that employer. Your approved 18-or-older answer can be reused across employers.
